## Quran structure cross-check

The generated `src/data/quranStructure.json` records a cross-check against [AlQuran Cloud's Uthmani Quran endpoint](https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/quran/quran-uthmani). Review [AlQuran Cloud's terms](https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions) before redistributing or modifying material derived from that service. Preserve orthography and diacritics, and attribute the source.

## Audio streams and contributed images

The audio JSON contains links to remote recordings; it does not include MP3 files. Reciters and providers may retain rights in those recordings. Every new image contributed to `public/discovery-images/` must include its creator, source, and permission for public redistribution in its pull request. Do not submit an image merely because it can be downloaded.
